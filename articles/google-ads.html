Google Ads: How to Capture Customers Who Are Already Searching for You

Unlike social ads that interrupt, Google Ads catches people while they're actively looking for what you sell. Here's how Search, Shopping and Display actually work.

● 6 min read ● Updated Sep 2026 ● BTech Marketing Wala

Social ads interrupt someone scrolling. Google Ads does the opposite — it shows up when someone is already typing what they need into a search bar, actively looking to buy or find a business like yours.

The three campaign types that matter most

  • Search — text ads that appear above organic results when someone searches a relevant term. This is usually where the highest-intent buyers are.
  • Shopping — product listings with an image and price, shown to people searching for a specific product. Essential for e-commerce.
  • Display & YouTube — visual ads shown across websites and video, better suited to building awareness or retargeting people who've already visited your site.

Most small businesses get the best return starting with Search, since it targets people who are already looking — then expand into Shopping or Display once that's dialled in.

Why keyword match type controls your budget

This is where most self-managed accounts waste money. Broad match keywords can trigger your ad for searches only loosely related to what you sell, burning budget on clicks that were never going to convert. Tightly chosen keywords with the right match types, backed by a strong negative keyword list, keep spend focused on people who actually want what you're offering.

Quality Score: the multiplier most people ignore

Google rewards relevant, well-structured campaigns with a lower cost per click through what's called Quality Score — based on your ad's relevance, expected click-through rate, and the experience on your landing page. Two advertisers can bid the same amount and pay very different prices depending on how well-optimised their account is.

This is also why the landing page a Google Ad sends traffic to matters as much as the ad itself — a fast, relevant, mobile-friendly page improves both your Quality Score and your conversion rate.

Frequently asked

How much do I need to spend on ads to see results?

Search or Meta Ads — which should I start with?

If people actively search for what you sell (a plumber, a specific product), start with Search. If you're building awareness for something people don't yet know they want, Meta Ads usually performs better first.
